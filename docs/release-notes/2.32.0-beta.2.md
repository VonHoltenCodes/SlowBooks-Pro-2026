# SlowBooks Pro 2.32.0-beta.2: release-note paragraph

This is the paragraph that leads the beta.2 notes (Connect to AI assistant
leads every 2.32 announcement), with the two notes that go with it.

**One AI assistant connection now reaches every company you add to it.** In
beta.1, Connect to AI assistant worked only in the company where you pressed
it, so an accountant with many client companies needed a connection for each
one. Now you connect once, then open each other company and press **Add** in
its Settings, under Connections, AI assistants. Each company gets its own
bookkeeper token, made in its own books, so every change the assistant makes
is in that company's audit log. The assistant names which company's books it
is working in on every call, and when it reaches more than one company it
must name one, or nothing is done. Only someone who is an administrator of
every company on the connection can show a sign-in code, add a company or
disconnect. A client company's own administrator sees only that company, can
take it off the connection, and does not see the names of the other
companies. If a company deactivates or demotes the administrator who added
it, its access ends at once. Treat the sign-in codes like a master key: an
assistant signed in with one can work every company on the connection. A
connection made in beta.1 keeps working as it is, with nothing to redo.

If your AI assistant stopped answering after the update, the person who
connected it is no longer an administrator; an administrator can connect it
again.

If you go back to beta.1 after adding companies, turn off "AI assistant
(relay)" in Settings → API tokens of each added company.
