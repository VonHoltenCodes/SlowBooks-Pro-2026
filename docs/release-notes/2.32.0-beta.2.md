# SlowBooks Pro 2.32.0-beta.2

**One AI assistant connection now reaches every company you add to it.** In
beta.1, Connect to AI assistant worked only in the company where you pressed
it, so an accountant with many client companies needed a connection for each
one. Now you connect once, then open each other company and press **Add** in
its Settings, under Connections, AI assistants. Each company gets its own
bookkeeper token, made in its own books, so every change the assistant makes
is in that company's audit log. The assistant names which company's books it
is working in on every call, and when it reaches more than one company it
must name one, or nothing is done. On a server, only someone who is an
administrator of every company on the connection can show a sign-in code, add
a company or disconnect. A client company's own administrator sees only that
company, can take it off the connection, and does not see the names of the
other companies. On the desktop, where only the open company answers, an
administrator of the open company can add it, and once it is on the
connection can show a sign-in code or disconnect. If a company deactivates
or demotes the administrator who added it, or that person's sign-in reaches
its end date, its access ends at once. When a company turns the assistant's token away, SlowBooks Pro deletes
its copy of that token from this computer at once, or a moment later when
another change to the connection is under way. Treat the sign-in codes
like a master key: an assistant signed in with one can work every company on
the connection. A connection made in beta.1 keeps working as it is, with
nothing to redo.

If your AI assistant stopped answering after the update, the person who
connected it is no longer an administrator there. An administrator of that
company (and, on a server serving several companies, of every other company
on the connection) opens its Settings, Connections, AI assistants and presses
**Add** again. The same rule now holds for a token you make yourself whose
name starts with "AI assistant" (SlowBooks Switchboard's, for example): it
stops working when the administrator who made it is deactivated or demoted.

The assistant is told why: the token "stopped working because the
administrator who made it is no longer an administrator of this company",
and Settings, API Tokens says the same on that token's row. A token you made
by hand before this version whose name starts with "AI assistant (relay)" is
now treated as the AI assistant connection's own: it works only from this
computer's own SlowBooks Pro, only while its maker is an administrator here,
and its name can't be changed. A program on the network that used one needs a
new token with another name.

If you go back to beta.1 after adding companies, turn off each "AI assistant
(relay)" token (" 2", " 3" and so on, when there are several) in Settings,
API Tokens of each added company.

**Long words stay on the page.** On every printed document, form and report,
a word longer than its space (a web address, a tracking number, a name typed
with no spaces) now breaks inside the word instead of pushing Qty, Rate,
Amount and the totals off the paper. A deposit slip's Received from, Check #
and Method, a statement's Number, a giving statement's Reference and a pay
application's Item stay on one line while they fit and wrap in their own
column when they do not, so an ordinary item number is never broken. A
check's payee (check on top), a pre-printed deposit slip's check numbers and
account name, and a mailing label's lines are cut with an ellipsis where
their room ends. On the General Ledger and the other reports, a reference
that is one long number (a wire's trace number) or a run of numbers with
commas wraps in its own column instead of printing over the description or
pushing Balance past the margin, and a large amount prints whole on one line
with its minus sign. The line at the top of a document's later pages wraps
inside the margin when its number is long, and a deposit's long account name
or Slip # wraps beside the date so Items stays on the page. A long last line
written without spaces (Japanese or Chinese, for example) is now divided
between its page and the totals' page like any other.
